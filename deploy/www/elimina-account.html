Deleting your account

Autochess Of Ages lets you delete your account and all associated data directly from the app:

  1. Open Autochess Of Ages and sign in (with Google or with email and password).
  2. From the main menu tap ⚙️ Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. The operation is immediate and irreversible.

What gets deleted: your profile (email, username, login credentials), statistics and leaderboard score, preferences, unlocked civilizations and login sessions.

What remains: results of online matches already completed, linked to a random identifier no longer connected to any profile; purchase transaction data, kept for the tax obligations required by law; technical server logs, automatically deleted after 30 days. See the privacy policy for details.

Single-player continues to work even without an account, as a guest.
