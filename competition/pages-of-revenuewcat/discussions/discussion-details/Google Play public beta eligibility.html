Google Play public beta eligibility?

Hi,

If I release a new app during the Submission Period under Google Play's public beta testing, does that qualify as eligible for the contest? A beta app would be downloadable from the Google Play store by the general public—it just requires an extra click to download.

Thanks,
Carter

  • 1 comment

  • Jaewoong Eum Manager   •   about 12 hours ago

    For Shipaton, Google Play Open Testing / public beta does not count as a public release, even if anyone can download it.

    To qualify for the general categories, the app needs to have its first production release on an eligible app store during the Shipaton submission period. A testing-track build alone isn’t enough.
